# Privacy Policy

**Short version: your subtitle files are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else.**

This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your files and what little information is collected when you visit subtitlefixer.com.

## Who runs this site

SubtitleFixer (subtitlefixer.com) is run by Kirill Luntovskii, based in Georgia. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the people running SubtitleFixer.

## Your subtitle files

**SubtitleFixer never uploads your subtitle files; all processing happens inside your browser.**

- When you add a file, it is read and processed by code running on your own device.
- The file's contents are not sent to our servers, to Cloudflare, to analytics, or to any other third party.
- We don't store your files. The fixed file is created in your browser and saved straight to your device when you download it.

You can check this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, go to the Network tab, and process a file. You won't see a request that contains your file.

## No account, no sign-up

**You don't need an account to use SubtitleFixer, and we never ask for your name or email address.**

We have no user accounts, no login, and no newsletter sign-up on the site.

## Cookies

**SubtitleFixer does not set any cookies of its own.**

We don't use advertising cookies or tracking cookies. The analytics tool we use (see below) is also designed to work without cookies.

## Analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

To understand which pages are useful and whether the site loads fast enough, we use [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/).

**Cloudflare Web Analytics is a privacy-first analytics tool that does not use cookies and, according to Cloudflare, does not collect visitors' personal data.**

In general terms, according to Cloudflare's documentation:

- A small script on the page sends Cloudflare information about the page view, such as the page address, the referring page (the site you came from), and page-load performance measurements.
- We see aggregated reports, such as how many page views a page had, which sites visitors came from, and broad breakdowns by country, browser, operating system and device type (desktop, mobile or tablet).
- The script does not use cookies or other browser storage (like localStorage) to track you.
- Cloudflare says it does not "fingerprint" visitors using their IP address, browser details or other data to track them for analytics.
- Cloudflare receives your IP address as part of the normal request, but says it discards it at the nearest data center and does not store it in its analytics databases or logs.

We cannot see who you are, and we do not try to identify individual visitors. The analytics never include the contents of your subtitle files.

For details, see Cloudflare's [Web Analytics documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/about/) and [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Hosting (Cloudflare)

SubtitleFixer is a static website hosted on Cloudflare. We don't run our own server, so we don't keep our own access logs.

Like any web host, Cloudflare needs basic technical information to deliver pages to you and to protect the site from abuse and attacks. This typically includes your IP address, browser type, the page you requested, and the time of the request. Cloudflare handles this information under its own [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

There are no ads and no affiliate tracking on SubtitleFixer at the moment. If that ever changes, we will update this policy before it happens.

## Privacy FAQ

### Are my subtitle files uploaded?

No. SubtitleFixer processes your subtitle files entirely in your browser, and the files are never uploaded to any server.

### Do I need an account to use SubtitleFixer?

No. All SubtitleFixer tools work without an account, sign-up or email address.

### Does SubtitleFixer use cookies?

No. SubtitleFixer does not set its own cookies, and the Cloudflare Web Analytics tool it uses is designed to work without cookies.

### Can SubtitleFixer see what's in my subtitles?

No. Because your files are processed on your own device, the contents of your subtitles are never sent to us or to anyone else.

### Does SubtitleFixer sell my data?

No. SubtitleFixer does not sell or share personal data, and it doesn't collect personal data about you in the first place.
